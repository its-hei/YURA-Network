Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Y.U.R.A. Scheduler Twitch Extension (“Extension”), provided by Heiyeshi, handles information.

1. Information collected by the developer

As of version 0.0.9, the Extension does not intentionally collect, store, or transmit viewers’ personal information to the developer.

The Extension is designed to display schedule information and a countdown. It does not require viewers to create an account, submit a form, or provide contact information.

2. Twitch Extension context

The Extension runs on Twitch and uses Twitch’s Extension environment. Twitch may process technical or account-related information independently under Twitch’s own privacy policy. The Extension does not currently use Twitch Extension identity information for its schedule features.

3. Third-party resources

The Extension may load category artwork and other resources from Twitch-operated content delivery systems. Requests to those systems are handled by Twitch and its infrastructure.

4. Cookies and local storage

The current Extension code does not create its own cookies and does not use browser local storage to build a viewer profile.

5. Future changes

If future versions introduce new data processing, authentication, analytics, or cloud features that affect viewers’ information, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are used publicly where required.

6. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to heiyeshi@gmail.com.
